# HIV vaccine BCR derived aggregate results

STATUS: SELECTED DERIVED AGGREGATE RESULTS.
Original sequence-level data are not included.

This directory contains 14 small aggregate tables
from an independent secondary computational analysis
of the published IAVI G002 B-cell receptor data.

Contents:
- 4 primary-analysis tables
- 4 sensitivity-analysis tables
- 5 AI benchmark summary tables
- 1 Figure 1A group-mean summary

Original study:
Willis et al., Science (2025)
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adr8382

Original data and software:
https://github.com/SchiefLab/G002-and-G003

Original archived release:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15425848

The original G002 sequence-level dataset is NOT
redistributed in this candidate package.

No participant identifiers, antibody sequences,
or individual participant-level plotting records
are intentionally included.

Figure 1A aggregate:
The included seven-position means are raw,
equally participant-weighted group means
derived from the frozen 15-participant profile.

The primary centered statistical contrasts
use each participant's mean across 65
complete-case IGHV1-2*02 positions.

The Figure 1A aggregate does not reproduce
individual participant scatter points.

The primary statistical results, sensitivity
results and exploratory antibody-language-model
benchmark findings remain unchanged.

LIMITATIONS:
The automated direct-identifier and sequence-field
screen found no flagged fields in the original
13-table candidate package.
